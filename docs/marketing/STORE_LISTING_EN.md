# Store Listing — EN

**Name:** N Keto Tracker
**Subtitle (≤30 chars):** Keto Journal & GKI Tracker (26)
**Description:**

N Keto Tracker is a private, open-source keto journal. Log meals,
macros and net carbs; record glucose + blood ketones (BHB) in the same
session and it computes GKI with a formula disclosure card. Weight & symptoms,
weekly meal plan and shopping list stay on your device. Your health data is
never sent anywhere and there is no account. The free version shows
non-personalized ads on a few non-clinical screens; a one-time Premium
purchase removes them. Light and dark themes, 18 languages.

This app is for general information only; not medical advice, diagnosis, or
treatment.
