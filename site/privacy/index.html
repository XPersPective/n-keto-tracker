Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026.

N Keto Tracker, developed by Crazy Penguin, works offline. We do not collect your personal or health data. The app has no account, advertising, analytics or in-app purchases.

Storage and deletion

Your profile, meals, measurements, weight, symptoms and notes are stored in an encrypted database on your device. The encryption key is held in the device’s secure storage. You can delete your records in the app or remove the app and its local data.

Exporting records

You can choose to export your records as JSON or CSV. You choose where to save or share the exported file. The app does not send your records automatically.

Intended use

N Keto Tracker is intended for adults and provides general information. It does not offer medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
